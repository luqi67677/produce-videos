How we run it

01

Plan weekly

Monday sets the week's three commitments. Anything that doesn't serve them waits — the plan is short so the week isn't.

02

Ship daily

Production sees every workday. Small releases keep risk small, reviews honest, and the demo always ready.

03

Audit always

Every change carries its evidence. The trail is written at ship time, not reconstructed at review time.
