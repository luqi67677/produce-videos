federation
/ˌfɛd·əˈreɪ·ʃən/ · noun
1

A union of partly self-governing entities under a central authority.

2

A network of city vision systems that share alerts — without ever sharing raw footage.

— as in: “Jakarta joins the federation without moving a single frame.”
