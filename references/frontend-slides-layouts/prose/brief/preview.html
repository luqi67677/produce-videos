Field Brief — Why Deployments Stall
Beacon

The cameras were never the hard part.

Every stalled deployment we audited had working hardware. What it lacked was an owner for the ninety days between installation and habit — the stretch where operators still distrust the alerts, dispatch still runs on radio memory, and one missed incident becomes the story everyone repeats. The cities that crossed that stretch assigned a named transition lead with authority over both rooms, and their usage curves never dipped. The hardware budget buys cameras; it is the transition lead that buys the outcome.

Audit base: 31 deployments, 2023–26, six provinces

Stall window: day 14–104 after install, median

With a named lead: 0 of 9 stalled
